#guide to playing the game

we'll skip over the building process as that is mentioned in the README.md.

Inside the game, as of right now you will just see white void as the background.
at the top left you will be greeted with the Title, and a grey box.
to play a map, simply hit enter and input the directory name of the desired map. see available
maps in the assets/maps or create one. wiki for that will be docs/guide2map.md.
to deselect the grey box, you need to empty the input and hit enter again

As soon as you're in the game, as of right now you will be greeted with a simple top down.
you can simply move around

to exit, as of right now the exit mechanic isnt implemented so you will have to force exit via close
button or alt+f4
